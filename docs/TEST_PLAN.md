## Reporting

- Tick a box when the check passes.
- For a failure, report the check, the system and the observed behavior, with the relevant lines of EDMC's log.
  - Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EDMarketConnector\logs\`
  - Linux: `~/.local/share/EDMarketConnector/EDMarketConnector.log`

## Getting the Build

1. Run `npm run deploy` from the branch being tested. It builds and merges the result into EDMC's plugins folder (native or Flatpak).
2. Restart EDMC. Every deploy requires a restart.
3. New files appear beside the plugin as the features are used: `carrier_route.json` (Carrier Route) and `mining_history.json` (Mining History), one record per commander.

## 1. Rare Goods Finder (Powerplay mode, Rares)

- [ ] The window shows **Distance**, **Arrival**, **Legality** and the other columns without cutting any off, and fits the screen.
- [ ] Distance is 0.0 ly for a rare in the commander's own system and grows down the list. It updates after a jump. A click on a heading sorts by number; a second click reverses the order.
- [ ] Compare Arrival (a station's distance from its star, in light seconds) with the game for a near station, a far one and Hutton Orbital.
- [ ] Legality: most rows read *Legal*. In a Federation or Alliance theocracy or a Prison Colony system, Lavian Brandy reads *Illegal here*, and selecting a row states where it is illegal. Compare with the station's Prohibited list on the system map.
- [ ] **Large pads only** keeps only large-pad stations.
  - **Only my Power** is disabled until the commander's Power is known. It then keeps the rares whose origin system the commander's Power controls. The status line shows Spansh being queried, ten systems at a time.
  - Both choices are remembered.
- [ ] **Copy system** puts the selected rare's origin system on the clipboard. Paste it into the galaxy map to confirm.

## 2. Mining (Mine mode)

- [ ] Space Mining window: **Last rock** follows each prospected rock. A core asteroid adds `core: <mineral>` and **Core asteroids found**. The limpet figure reads `(N on board)` and matches the cargo.
- [ ] Space Mining window, Settings card: enter a target commodity and a percentage. A rock at or above the percentage reads `target`.
- [ ] Accept a mining mission. **Mission needs** shows its tons against the hold and changes as the hold changes.
- [ ] Flight Alerts (Settings → Overlays → Flight Alerts, Mining alerts ticked): the Test Card shows the green core lines.
  - In a ring, a core asteroid and a rich rock give a green card for about 30 seconds.
  - Low limpets and a nearly full hold give amber cards.
  - No card shows while the commander is not mining.
  - The sound plays if it is ticked.
- [ ] **Mining History** (the Dashboard button): the Overview, Commodities, Rings and Runs tabs fill from the journals and sort. Compare the totals with the journals.
- [ ] **Book** in the Space Mining window opens the **Space Mining Book**.
  - It lists rings from the journals with hotspots per mineral, class, reserve level, tons mined and last mined.
  - Test a note, a favorite, **Find**, **Add ring** and **Copy system**. After an EDMC restart the notes remain.
  - **Book** in the Surface Mining window opens the **Surface Mining Book**, with a **From your journals** group (bodies with mining locations and the tons refined on them).
- [ ] **This system** (in both books):
  - In a system the commander has mined, it goes to the ring or body. In a system not mined, it reads `You have not worked this system yet.`
  - On the first open of each book, a notice states that the journals are being read, and the list does not flicker while it grows.
- [ ] The Surface Compass, the Surface Mining Book's stops and Market research, and Share line work. Their checks are in [TODO.md](TODO.md).

## 3. Carrier Route (Route Planner window → Carrier route)

- [ ] The carrier block and the boxes fill in from the journal (the carrier's location, the tritium in its tank, the space used) and match Carrier Management. If they read not known, open Carrier Management once.
- [ ] Enter a destination and press **Generate route**. Compare the jumps and the tritium with Spansh's Fleet Carrier Router page for the same carrier. The first line states the tritium required at the start.
- [ ] **Copy next system** puts the next system on the clipboard. Paste it to confirm. Schedule the jump in the game. When the carrier arrives, the row turns green and the next row is marked. **Mark next as reached** does the same by hand.
- [ ] Restart EDMC. The route is restored. A second commander sees no route.
- [ ] With a squadron carrier, choose it in the Carrier box and plot a route.

## 5. Added 2026-10-23

Run on **Windows** and on **Linux**. Where the two differ, the line states so.

- [ ] **Every overlay is off after the first start of this build.**
  - Restart EDMC. The log has one line, `All WFMT overlays reset to disabled (one-time), including the saved choices of N commander(s)`, and no overlay card shows for any commander.

  - Start an expedition, jump or scan, press Stop, wait a minute, start it again from the Route tab. The clock continues from where it stopped.

- [ ] **The screen size.** Settings → Overlays → Overlay Placement states where the size came from.
  - With the game's files or the overlay running, it names them.
  - Rename the overlay's log (EDMCModernOverlay's `overlay_client.log`) and the game's `DisplaySettings.xml` so that neither is found, and press **Detect screen again**. The text states that the primary display is used, with the real resolution. Restore the files afterwards.
  - Windows: the size is the hardware resolution, not the scaled one (3840 x 2160 at 125% reads 3840 x 2160). Linux: report the session type (Wayland or X11) and whether `xrandr` is installed.
  - On an ultrawide or triple screen, open the placement preview and confirm that no box is off the screen or on top of another.
- [ ] **Long names in the tables.** In the Missions, Source commodities, Ship Builds and My Codex windows, a long faction, station, ship, role or site name shows whole at a wide size and is cut with an ellipsis at a narrow one. The window does not grow wider than its size.

The Colonization page, the Sites window, the fleet carrier column and the overlay positions are unchanged. A body or system whose name contains the letters of an old spelling inside a word (the old spelling of tons or analyzed) keeps its name.
