# Features at a glance

This page lists every feature in two ways: by task, and by mode with the controls to use. Each mode guide gives the detail and ends with what the game does and does not report. Related pages: the [README](../../README.md) and the [guide index](README.md).

### Powerplay ([guide](powerplay.md))

**Controls:** the panel shows live numbers. **Report** opens the Sessions window, **Rares** opens the Rare Goods Finder, **Rescan** rebuilds from the journals. The CP ratios, the Copy Progress format and the journal scan depth are on the **Settings** tab of the Sessions window.

| Feature | What it does | How to use it | Deep dive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live merits and Control Points | Merits and estimated CP for this system, this session and the whole Powerplay cycle | Automatic; the panel updates during play | [Using it](powerplay.md#using-it) |
| Sessions window | Tabs for the current session, systems, cycles, days, megaships and history | **Report**; drop-downs pick an earlier cycle; **Copy Progress**, **Reset Current System** and **Reset Session** are in the header | [The Sessions window](powerplay.md#the-sessions-window) |
| Systems tab | A tab per system with its standing and what was earned; pin up to five | **☆ Pin**, **Show a system** and **Add system**, **× Close tab** | [The Systems tab](powerplay.md#the-systems-tab) |
| Catch-up | Rebuilds cycles from the journals so that play without EDMC is counted | Automatic at start-up; **Rescan** to rebuild | [Catching up](powerplay.md#catching-up-from-your-journals) |
| Megaships | The megaships whose datalink was scanned this cycle, with a count and where | Drop out of supercruise at the megaship, then scan; see the **Megaships** tab | [Megaships](powerplay.md#megaships) |
| Powerplay overlay card | The commander's Power, rank, merits and the cycle's merits, the system's state and holder, what merits there count as, and what was earned there this session (with estimated CP), on the game screen; displays **No Powerplay Detected in system** where there is none; optionally switches to the system being jumped to (asks Spansh) | **Settings → WFMT → Overlays → Powerplay** | [The overlay card](powerplay.md#the-overlay-card) |
| Rare Goods Finder | The rare commodities nearest the current system: distance to the origin system, the station's distance from its star, pad, legality, and which Power controls the system | **Rares**; **Show nearest** and **Apply**, **Large pads only**, **Only my Power**, **Copy system**; double-click opens Inara | [Rare Goods Finder](powerplay.md#rare-goods-finder) |

### Mining ([guide](mining.md))

**Controls:**

- **Space Mining** and **Surface Mining** open each page's window; the panel shows a short summary of both.
- **Book** (in each window's header) opens the Space Mining Book from Space Mining and the Surface Mining Book from Surface Mining.
- **Dashboard** (in each window's header) opens Mining History.
- The windows are the second-screen live view (**Mark center**, **Mark border**, **Add hotspot** and **Import/export** are in the Surface Mining one).
- **Hotspots**, **Prices** and **Reserves** are the lookups.
- The display switches, target commodities and tuning are on the Settings card at the top of each window; the lookups that contact other services, the archive option and the simulation are under **Settings → WFMT → Mining**.

| Feature | What it does | How to use it | Deep dive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Space Mining window | Live stats for the run: asteroids prospected and cracked, the last rock and any core asteroids, limpets aboard, what was refined and the rate, and what the mining missions still need | Automatic during mining | [What each page shows](mining.md#what-each-page-shows) |
| Surface Mining window | The Rhino's cargo, what was refined and transferred, raw materials, the last DSS scan, known hotspots here and a minimap of the ground driven | Launch the Rhino; tick *Show coverage minimap* on the window's Settings card for the map | [What each page shows](mining.md#what-each-page-shows) |
| Space Mining Book | Every ring the journals show hotspots for or that were mined at: hotspots by mineral, class, reserve level, tons mined and when, the note and a favorite; fills itself from the journals | **Book** in the Space Mining window's header; **Find**, **Favorites only**, **Add ring**, **This system**, **Copy system**, **Save note**, **Favorite** | [The Space Mining Book](mining.md#the-space-mining-book) |
| Surface Mining Book | The catalog of Rhino deposits: tons mined, tons left (an estimate), dated Depleted marks, Rhino stops, market research, what pays (the commodities ranked by the best price Spansh shows), sharing one deposit as a line; plus the bodies the journals show mining on | **Book** in the Surface Mining window's header; **Add hotspot**, **Mark depleted**, **Market research**, **What pays**, **Share line**, **Paste spot**, **This system** | [The Surface Mining Book](mining.md#the-surface-mining-book) |
| Mining History | Every mining run in the journals added up: tons, tons per hour, commodities, rings, prospecting quality, limpets and what the sales paid, including runs made with EDMC closed | **Dashboard** in either mining window's header; sortable tabs | [Mining History](mining.md#mining-history) |
| Live windows | Windows for a second screen: Surface Mining has a radar centered on the Rhino (range rings, ground driven, marked locations, saved deposits, the nearest one) with the panel's lines and the Mark center, Mark border, Add hotspot and Import/export controls; Space Mining has the live numbers | **Space Mining** and **Surface Mining** (or the Live shortcut); the buttons under the radar set its reach | [The live window](mining.md#the-live-window) |
| Mark a mining location | The center and border of a surface mining signal, drawn on the minimap with its radius and how much of its edge has been driven | **Mark center** at the middle, **Mark border** at the edge, both in the live window | [Marking a mining location](mining.md#marking-a-mining-location) |
| Save and share hotspots | Save the current position, import or export the list | **Add hotspot** saves; **Import/export** imports or exports (both in the live window) | [The buttons](mining.md#the-buttons) |
| Mining alerts | A card for a core asteroid or a rock rich in the target commodity; warnings for low limpets and a nearly full hold while mining | The target commodities on the Settings card of the Space Mining window; **Settings → WFMT → Overlays → Flight Alerts** to switch them on | [Flight Alerts](always-on.md#flight-alerts) |
| Mining overlay and archive | Live stats on the game screen; an optional JSON file per completed run | **Settings → WFMT → Overlays → Mining**; *Archive completed mining runs* | [Settings](mining.md#settings) |
| Mining radar over the game | The Surface Live radar drawn on the game screen while the Rhino is out (WFMT's built-in overlay, Windows) | **Settings → WFMT → Overlays → Mining**: *Show the Mining radar over the game*, its size and opacity, **Test radar** | [The radar over the game](mining.md#the-radar-over-the-game) |
| Opt-in lookups | Nearby hotspots and best price (Spansh), ring reserve level (EDSM) | Tick them under **Settings → WFMT → Mining**, then **Hotspots**, **Prices**, **Reserves** | [The buttons](mining.md#the-buttons) |

### Trade ([guide](trade.md))

**Controls:**

- **Session**, **Routes**, **Market** and **History** buttons each open a window.
- Session: **Reset**, **Save session**, **History**, **Rebuild**.
- Routes: **Find routes**, **Cancel**, **Hops**, **Round trip**, **Copy next system**.
- Market: **Sell**/**Buy**, **Near me**/**Galaxy**, **Best picks**, **Price finder**.
- Watch list: **Watch**, **Refresh**.
- The Spansh lookups and the carrier choice are under **Settings → WFMT → Trade**; the route requirements, the Near me radius and ship size are set in the Routes and Market windows, and *Count fines and bounties* in Session.

| Feature | What it does | How to use it | Deep dive |
|---|---|---|---|

| Market and Best picks | Where to sell or buy a commodity; the closest, best local and cheapest station | Pick **Sell** or **Buy**, type a commodity, **Near me** or **Galaxy**; **Best picks** | [Market](trade.md#market), [Best picks](trade.md#best-picks) |
| Watch list | The watched commodities with the best buy and sell price | **Watch**; add a commodity; **Refresh** | [Watch list](trade.md#watch-list) |
| Fleet and squadron carrier | Cargo and tritium kept up to date from the journal | Open Carrier Management once in the game | [The fleet carrier](trade.md#your-fleet-carrier-and-squadron-carrier) |
| Two computers | How Trade records behave on Windows and Linux | **Rebuild** on the second machine | [Using Trade on two computers](trade.md#using-trade-on-two-computers) |

### Missions ([guide](missions.md))

**Controls:** the panel lists each type with its count; **Mission types** opens the pages in a window, with the six display switches on its **Display** card. **Missions window** and **Find sources** are at the bottom of the panel. The Missions overlay is under **Settings → WFMT → Overlays → Missions**.

| Feature | What it does | How to use it | Deep dive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every mission in one view | Pages by type (in the Mission types window); grouped kill-progress bars for massacre and settlement raids; one card per mission otherwise | **Mission types** | [Using it](missions.md#using-it) |
| Missions window | Every mission with progress, reward, time left, where to hand it in, and pinning | **Missions window**; pin to keep one on the overlay | [The Missions window](missions.md#the-missions-window) |
| Mission description window | Everything the journal says about one mission: objective, target, cargo, passengers, reward, influence and reputation | Click a mission's card in the Mission types window | [The Mission description window](missions.md#the-mission-description-window) |
